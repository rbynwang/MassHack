open app/story.html           # this page, offline

    The seven protocol messages (agent card, ask, offer, request, response, assignment, receipt) are published as JSON Schemas in protocol/schemas/. Every message is an Ed25519-signed envelope.

    Synthetic and simulated members are labeled. Battery sizes, prices paid and tariffs are assumptions; savings are simulation results, not measured outcomes. Data: HouseZero (Harvard CGBC, CC BY 4.0, DOIs 10.6084/m9.figshare.30260233.v1 and 10.6084/m9.figshare.32790984.v1), City of Boston Analyze Boston (Central Library electricity, PDDL), ISO New England, National Grid (Massachusetts Electric) class load shapes, NREL ComStock templates.
