Every building gets an agent. Every neighborhood gets a network.

Commonwatt turns a Massachusetts neighborhood's homes, businesses and public buildings into an energy commons. Each building's agent keeps its owner's limits private, negotiates with a coordinator when the grid asks for help, and gets paid against a signed receipt.
