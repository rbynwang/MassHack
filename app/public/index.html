Boston buildings that think ahead together.

Each building forecasts its own load from the weather, plans its battery for its own bill, and offers the extra help it can give. A coordinator pools those offers when the grid needs help, a trust layer enforces every member's limits in code and signs a receipt for each action, and the money is split by contribution.

6 What if tomorrow is hotter?

The world model imagines, the coordinator changes its actions.

Same summer peak day, with the weather forecast shifted by −10, −5, 0 or +3 °C. Every member re-forecasts, the world model imagines each building mode, batteries re-plan, and the coordinator re-assigns. A hotter forecast can trigger a heat warning: cooling centers then lock half their battery and refuse building-mode control.
